# Anti-patterns

Reject or redesign:

- dual y-axes;
- 3D bars, pies, or perspective;
- unlabeled axes or missing units;
- alphabetical ordering when ranked comparison is intended;
- rainbow magnitude scales;
- red/green-only status encoding;
- changing entity colors after filtering;
- trend lines without a stated method or enough data;
- pie charts with many slices;
- maps used only because a location field exists;
- cumulative values shown as independent bars;
- truncated baselines that amplify small changes;
- decorative motion that delays or hides information;
- labels on every point when they collide;
- dashboards with no visible question or takeaway.
